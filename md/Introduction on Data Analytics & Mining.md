### Data Analytics (DA)
• Data analytics is the process of examining data sets
in order to ==draw conclusions about the information
they contain==. 

### Advanced types of Data Analytics  
*Data Mining*  
* involves sorting through large data sets to identify trends, patterns and relationships  

*Predictive Analytics*  
* seeks to predict customer behavior, equipment failures and other future events  

*Machine Learning*  
* an artificial intelligence technique that uses automated algorithms to churn through data sets more quickly than data scientists can do via conventional analytical modeling.  
* All Machine Learning Models Explained in 5 Minutes | Types of ML Models Basics:  
https://youtu.be/yN7ypxC7838

*Big data analytics*  
* applies data mining, predictive analytics and machine learning tools to sets of big data that often contain
unstructured and semi-structured data.  

*Text mining*  
* provides a means of analyzing documents, emails and other textbased content.

### *Data mining* (Knowledge discovery in databases)
The computational process of discovering patterns in
large data sets involving of ==artificial intelligence==, ==machine learning==, ==statistics==, and ==database systems==.  

### *Machine Learning*
> “Gives computers the ability to learn without being
explicitly programmed” -- Arthur Samuel, 1959.  

![](https://i.imgur.com/xCrz20N.png =50%x)![](https://i.imgur.com/Y4yGxAp.png =50%x)

* Machine learning develops algorithms for making predictions from data  
* ==Machine Learning = Model + Parameter + Learner==  
* Type of Tasks  
    * Supervised Learning(監督式學習)
    * Classification (分類)
    * Unsupervised Learning(非監督式學習, 分群)
    * Reinforcement Learning(強化學習)  

### Data Mining/Analytics Potential Applications  
* ==Data analysis and decision support==
    * Market analysis and management
        * Target marketing, customer relationship management(CRM), market basket analysis, cross selling, market segmentation
    * Risk analysis and management
        * Forecasting, customer retention, competitive analysis
    * Fraud detection and detection of unusual patterns (outliers)
        * “Whoscall”
